---
name: allagma-protocol
description: Define a frozen protocol with controls and held-out evaluation.
---

Record hypotheses, independent variables, baselines, metrics, exclusions, uncertainty calculation, failure criteria, seeds and data partitions in the protocol. Identify editable and fixed boundaries. Define completion and resource stops before confirmation.

Qualify the study-owned runner and evaluator against known-answer controls in a pilot. Confirmation seeds and data must be distinct from discovery and pilot. Record amendments as a new revision with reasons and affected runs; never overwrite earlier results. A new protocol starts a new campaign unless an explicit amendment contract establishes a valid handoff.
