# A starting point for validation

For independent random variables with expectation zero and variance one, the
sample mean of n observations has expectation zero and variance 1/n. The
identity MSE(estimator) = variance(estimator) + bias(estimator)² gives a way to
check a simulator. Derive the implications for the brief's estimators before
looking at confirmation results.

This original mathematical note is supplied as a known-answer reference, not
an empirical result or claim of novelty. Allagma contributors, 2026. MIT.
