# Toy paper outline

This example uses only the completed default toy's frozen protocol, derivation,
raw-data manifest, analysis and claim ledger. No external literature was searched.
It adds a publication revision; it does not change the experiment or its report.

- Abstract: identify the synthetic comparison, seed unit and actual paired result;
  distinguish the workflow demonstration from scientific novelty.
- Introduction: pose the question about adding a constant to an unbiased mean.
- Related work: explain provided-only coverage and the study-owned derivation;
  no invented external reference or claim of comprehensive coverage.
- Methods: separate pilots and confirmation, pairing and failure exclusions;
  explain the normal-approximation interval and its limits.
- Results: resolve numbers and the average claim from retained JSON; retain the
  counterexample to a universal ordering and the leave-one-seed-out sensitivity.
- Discussion: relate the average result to the algebraic expectation without
  generalizing to every seed or to language-model performance.
- Limitations: narrow distribution, sample size, approximate uncertainty,
  unsearched literature and self-review scope.

The final sections use paragraphs. The configuration contains result locators,
not replacement measurements. The ordinary toy's SVG remains in its report;
this small paper uses a numerical table and needs no figure-conversion software.
