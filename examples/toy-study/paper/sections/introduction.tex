Does adding a constant to an unbiased sample mean increase its expected squared
error? This question has an algebraic answer and a small executable example.
For the zero-mean distribution used here, the sample mean has expected squared
error $1/n$. Adding a constant $b$ gives $1/n+b^2$, since the mean is unbiased
and the cross term has expectation zero. The frozen study-owned derivation
records this argument. The realized estimate from a finite set of seeds need
not equal the expectation.
