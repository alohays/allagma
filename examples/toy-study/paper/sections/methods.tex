The frozen protocol separates two known-answer pilot controls from
\AllagmaValue{seeds} confirmation seeds. Each confirmation seed generates
\AllagmaValue{samples} Rademacher observations using Python's pseudorandom
generator. Both estimators use the same observations and target zero. We
subtract the sample mean's squared error from the shifted estimator's squared
error within each seed, then average the paired differences across seeds.
Positive differences favor the sample mean.

The standard error is the sample standard deviation of the seed differences
divided by the square root of the number of confirmation seeds. The approximate
95\% interval is the mean plus or minus 1.96 standard errors. Leave-one-seed-out
analysis recomputes the mean after omitting each seed once. Pilots and unsuccessful
attempts do not enter confirmation analysis; retained retries are attempts to
complete the same seed, not additional independent observations.
