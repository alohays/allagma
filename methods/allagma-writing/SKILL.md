---
name: allagma-writing
description: Write an evidence-linked manuscript with explicit limitations.
---

Read the reference index, then the source passages for every citation. Use
verified bibliographic entries and record writing decisions in the literature
map. A correct citation does not prove its associated claim. Preserve conflicting
findings and coverage limits. Humanize prose without changing scientific meaning,
numbers, uncertainty, citations or attribution.

Create a ClaimRecord for each empirical conclusion, connecting supporting and contradicting evidence, scope and limitations. Verify all referenced digests and dependencies. An evidence change makes a dependent claim stale until reanalysis and review.

Write a manuscript with question, methods, results, limitations and bibliography. Generate tables and empirical numbers from verified analysis outputs. Check bibliographic metadata separately from support. Identify any unperformed experiment or unreviewed inference. The requested deliverable is a draft; submission requires its own authorization.
