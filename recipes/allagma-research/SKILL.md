---
name: allagma-research
description: Run the research workflow using portable artifact handoffs.
---

Read `recipe.json` for roles, data handoffs, branches and stopping conditions. Resolve every method through the selected campaign bundle. Execute sequentially unless an explicitly authorized adapter supplies parallel work.

Follow scope (including critical reference research), protocol, pilot, campaign, analysis, manuscript and audit. Carry the reference index, located source support and coverage limits through every phase and resumed session. Failed pilot qualification stops confirmation. Resume interrupted execution with new attempt IDs. A resource or external-input stop produces a partial report. Negative or inconclusive results are valid research outcomes. An audit revision request returns to the affected phase; cap revisions at the configured limit.

The replication recipe adds a second independent reanalysis directory before audit; it reuses the same methods and contracts.
