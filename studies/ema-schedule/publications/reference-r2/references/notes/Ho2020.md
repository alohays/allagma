# Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models

Defines the DDPM family and supplies context for evaluating averaged diffusion weights.

Verified metadata: 2020; Ho, Jonathan, Jain, Ajay, Abbeel, Pieter. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.11239v2). Checked 9 October 2026.

Section 3, Algorithms 1 and 2; Appendix B, PDF pp. 14-15.

The paper specifies noise-prediction diffusion training and reverse sampling. Appendix B reports EMA with decay 0.9999 without a decay sweep.

Assessment: supports. EMA is part of this influential DDPM implementation, but its reported performance does not identify the marginal effect of EMA or an optimal decay.

Scope: Image-generation results use much larger networks and FID; they do not establish an EMA effect for a tiny 2D denoiser or compare the decays used here.

Study consequence: Cite DDPM for the model family and reported EMA usage, not proof that EMA must improve this study.
