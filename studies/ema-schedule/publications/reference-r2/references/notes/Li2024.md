# Switch EMA: A Free Lunch for Better Flatness and Sharpness

Adds explicit counterexamples to universal EMA-benefit language and describes a different intervention that changes training weights.

Verified metadata: 2024; Li, Siyuan, Liu, Zicheng, Tian, Juanxi, Wang, Ge, Wang, Zedong, Jin, Weiyang, Wu, Di, Tan, Cheng, Lin, Tao, Liu, Yang, Sun, Baigui, Li, Stan Z.. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.09240v2). Checked 9 October 2026.

Section 4.1, Tables 9 and 10 (PDF p. 9); Section 3 definition of switching.

Some reported video-prediction and regression cells are worse with ordinary EMA than their basic baseline. The proposed switching procedure replaces training parameters at specified intervals.

Assessment: contradicts. Ordinary EMA improves every evaluated task endpoint.

Scope: Switch EMA feeds averaged parameters back into training. It does not share an unchanged raw trajectory with ordinary EMA, and the reported cross-task comparisons do not test this study's fixed protocol.

Study consequence: Avoid repeating the title's universal-benefit framing as an established scientific conclusion.
