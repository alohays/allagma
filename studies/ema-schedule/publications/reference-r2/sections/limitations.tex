There are only four independent seeds per dataset, two synthetic
distributions and one small denoiser. Confidence intervals are wide,
multiple comparisons are numerous and generated samples do not increase
the number of independent training runs. A zero-containing interval does
not establish equivalence. SW1 on these two-dimensional datasets is not
directly comparable with image-scale FID or classification accuracy.

The study does not test more EMA windows, SWA, post-hoc averaging,
parameter-switching methods, different architectures or alternative
optimizers. It keeps the diffusion-noise schedule fixed and does not
separate the terminal optimizer rate from the rest of its schedule. Mode
coverage reaches a ceiling and cannot rule out incorrect component masses.
The finite-sample SW1 estimates are not debiased by subtracting a reference
floor.

This publication revision reuses completed scientific outputs. Its
targeted literature map is retrospective and not an exhaustive systematic
review. The numerical checks in this revision recompute summaries from
retained seed-level measurements; they do not rerun training or extract
metrics anew from all saved samples. Earlier retained controller checks
provide the separate saved-state replay evidence. The agent-assisted
scientific critique and prose review are provisional and are not independent
scientific peer review.
