\subsection{Retained design and implementation}
The experiment crossed two datasets (two moons and an eight-component Gaussian
mixture, GMM8), two terminal durations (5,000 and 10,000 optimizer updates), and
two learning-rate policies. The constant policy used $\eta_0=0.0003$. For
duration $D$, update $k$ under cosine used
\begin{equation}
\eta_k(D)=\frac{\eta_0}{2}\left[1+\cos\!\left(\frac{\pi(k-1)}{D}\right)\right].
\end{equation}
The final applied rate is slightly above zero, while the scheduled rate after
$D$ updates is zero. The policies differ over the entire training path; the
experiment does not isolate an effect caused only by the terminal rate.

Each constant-rate trajectory supplies both checkpoints. Cosine-5k and
cosine-10k are separate trajectories initialized with the same weights and
matched training-input prefixes. The \AllagmaValue{cells} evaluation cells
therefore come from \AllagmaValue{trajectories} trajectories totaling
\AllagmaValue{updates} optimizer updates, rather than one independent
trajectory per checkpoint. Within each trajectory, the two EMA copies start
at the initial raw weights and update after every optimizer step:
\begin{equation}
\bar\theta_t^{(\beta)}=\beta\bar\theta_{t-1}^{(\beta)}+(1-\beta)\theta_t,
\qquad \beta\in\{0.99,0.999\}.
\end{equation}
Neither EMA copy changes the raw training trajectory. There is no warmup,
bias correction or sparse update rule.

The supplied residual denoiser has 296,450 parameters, 128-dimensional
embeddings, width 256 and three residual blocks. Its lineage is the pinned
AI-Scientist 2D diffusion template \cite{Sakana2025}. The study retained
AdamW with weight decay 0.01, batch size 256, gradient-norm clipping at 0.5,
and MPS float32 training. It used a fixed 100-step cosine diffusion-noise
schedule and clipped predicted clean samples to $[-6,6]$. Adaptations include
the dataset generators, evaluation metric and constant every-step EMA; the
study is not a reproduction of the upstream template's reported results.

\subsection{Pairing, evaluation and uncertainty}
The independent unit is a training seed within a dataset, with
$n=\AllagmaValue{seeds}$. Confirmation seeds were 4001--4004 for moons and
5001--5004 for GMM8, separate from the pilot seeds. Each seed used 100,000
training draws, 2,048 held-out draws and 2,048 generated draws per model state.
Initial weights, replacement minibatch indices, training noise, diffusion
timesteps and evaluation randomness were matched across the relevant
conditions. Actual arrays and prefix hashes were retained. The held-out and
generation streams were separate from training.

The primary metric is empirical Sliced Wasserstein-1 (SW1), computed in
float64 over 128 fixed projection directions with projection seed 7321.
Lower values indicate better agreement with the held-out sample. For each
seed, define the relative effect
\begin{equation}
\Delta_{p,D,\beta}=\operatorname{SW1}(\mathrm{EMA}_{p,D,\beta})
                   -\operatorname{SW1}(\mathrm{raw}_{p,D}).
\end{equation}
Negative values favor EMA. Schedule contrasts are
$\Delta_{\mathrm{cosine},D,\beta}-\Delta_{\mathrm{constant},D,\beta}$;
duration contrasts compare 10k with 5k at a fixed policy. The interaction is
the 10k schedule contrast minus its 5k counterpart. Absolute raw and EMA
means are retained alongside these differences.

The analysis reports paired means, unadjusted two-sided 95\% Student $t$
intervals with three degrees of freedom, individual seed values,
leave-one-seed-out means and exact two-sided sign-flip $p$-values. With four
paired seeds, the minimum attainable sign-flip $p$-value is 0.125. Generated
points, projections, retries and shared checkpoints are not additional
independent replicates. The intervals are descriptive and imprecise at this
sample size; no multiplicity-adjusted confirmatory claim is made.

GMM8 coverage counts a mode when at least 21 of the 2,048 generated draws
lie within distance 0.45 of their nearest mixture center. Coverage is a
secondary diagnostic and does not establish correct component masses or
distributional equality. The frozen protocol and selected scientific inputs
are supplied with the paper's ancillary provenance.
